Estate Administration & Probate

Losing someone is hard enough. Let us help with the paperwork.

Winding up someone's affairs is complex, lengthy and emotionally draining, and executors are personally liable for getting it right. We can act on your behalf for some or all of the work, so you can focus on your family.

Speak to us: 01384 883 883 Send a message

On average, estate administration takes 8–9 months, and complex estates can take much longer and run up significant costs.

Your executors, named in the Will, are legally and financially accountable throughout. You don't have to do it alone.

What's involved

An executor's responsibilities

From dealing with the courts and HMRC through to final distribution, we can take on as much or as little of this as you'd like.

Pick and choose. Some families want us to handle everything, while others just need help with the grant of probate or the tax forms. Fees are agreed up front, so you know where you stand.

  1. Register the death & arrange the funeral

  2. Inform banks, utilities and organisations

  3. Identify and value all assets and debts

  4. Complete tax forms, including Inheritance Tax

  5. Obtain the grant of probate

  6. Collect assets, pay debts & taxes on time

  7. Set up any Trusts in the Will & transfer property

  8. Produce full accounts & distribute the estate

Planning ahead

Make things easier for your own executors

The best gift you can give your family is a clear, up-to-date Will, stored safely, with the right Trusts in place.

  • A Family Protection Trust can mean no probate on your home
  • Storage & Aftercare gives your executors free advice while they administer your estate
  • Naming reserve or professional executors avoids problems if someone can't act
