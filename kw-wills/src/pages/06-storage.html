Your questions

How it works

How much does it cost?

A small annual fee. Please ask for our current price list. Payments are collected by annual Direct Debit through London & Zurich on the 1st or 15th of the month, and show on your statement as “Will Storage”. You're protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee and can stop at any time.

Will the price go up?

Fees are reviewed periodically. Since 2014 they've only gone up twice, and only for new clients. If your rate ever changed, you'd be told in writing in advance.

What if I forget where my Will is?

You'll receive certificates to give to family or friends, so they know a Will exists, where it's held and how to retrieve it. All stored Wills are also registered with The National Will Register (0330 100 3660), so they can always be found.

How do I get my documents back?

At any time. Call {{PHONE_1}}, email {{OFFICE_EMAIL}} or write to King Will's Wills Ltd, {{ADDRESS}}.

Who else can retrieve them?

For your protection, only your executors can retrieve your Will, and only with a death certificate. Your attorneys can only retrieve your LPAs with your written consent or a GP's letter confirming loss of mental capacity.

How does the update discount work?

50% off if your Will can be updated by post or email, or 25% off if a consultation is needed. The discount applies to the Will itself. Anything already drafted, such as a Trust, isn't charged again, and new services are charged at the price at that time.
