How your appointments work

{{icon:chat}}

1 · Getting to know you

Around 1½–2 hours, at your home or on Zoom, in office hours, evenings or weekends.

  • Understand what's important to you
  • Assess your circumstances
  • Advise you, and quote a fixed fee up front
  • Gather what's needed to draft your documents
{{icon:pen}}

2 · Signing & explaining

Around ½–1 hour. Unlike many firms, we come back to:

  • Return your completed documents
  • Explain everything in full
  • Answer any questions
  • Supervise signing and witnessing so it's all valid

We'll also organise any extra signatures needed from people named in your documents.

Couples: both of you need to be present throughout. Witnesses are only needed for the last 5–10 minutes of the second visit: two independent adults who aren't beneficiaries (or their partners). Neighbours, friends and colleagues are ideal.

The small print, in plain English

Fees, payment & your rights

How do fees and payment work?

All fees are fixed and quoted in full before you decide anything, with no hidden charges. If extra work is ever needed (which is rare), we'll ask you first.

A 50% deposit is due after the first appointment, with the balance on return of your documents or after 28 days, whichever comes first. You can also pay in full up front. We accept bank transfer, card or cheque, and instalments may be possible, so just ask.

Is there a cooling-off period?

Yes. For non-commercial appointments you have 14 days after instructing us, and any payment is refunded in full. If you ask us to start sooner and then cancel within the 14 days, work already done would be charged.

Are there any extra charges?

To keep prices competitive we operate a fair-usage policy. We may charge a fixed fee for additional appointments, advice-only appointments, out-of-hours or emergency appointments, but you'll always know in advance.

Are you insured?

Yes. Professional indemnity insurance of £2 million (upgradable to £5 million) through BEAM, and £5 million public liability insurance through Konsileo.

Why do you need ID?

Money laundering regulations require us to verify each client's identity. It also protects you from fraud.
