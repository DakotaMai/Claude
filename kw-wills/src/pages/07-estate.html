Estate Administration & Probate

Losing someone is hard enough. Let us help with the rest.

Probate can be complex, lengthy and emotionally challenging. We can act on your behalf for some or all of it, from the courts and tax offices through to final distribution.

On average, estate administration takes 8–9 months, and longer if complex.

Over 500,000 people die each year in the UK, and over 60% (around 1,000 a day) don't have a Will. Executors named in a Will are legally and financially liable for the process.
