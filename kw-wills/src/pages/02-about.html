My philosophy

Why I do what I do

I'm passionate about helping people sort out their Wills and plan their estate, because I see the difference it makes to their peace of mind. Many people put it off “one of these days”, but worry about not having provided for their loved ones.

I offer a friendly, no-obligation consultation, online or at your home or workplace, often in the evening or at weekends. I personally draft documents in house, so the service is consistent and nothing gets “lost in translation”. I'll go through every detail and answer every question. No job is too big or small, and I've even turned a Will around in a couple of hours in an emergency.

One of the first questions I ask at an appointment is “Why a Will, why now?” The most common answer: “In case anything happens to me / us!”

“What matters to me is that my clients are happy. And they are.”

The STEP Will Code

The standards you can expect

A set of principles that protect you when a STEP member prepares your Will.

{{icon:chat}}

Openness

  • Clear about qualifications, costs and tax implications
  • A draft for you to approve first
  • Your identity confirmed to protect you from fraud
  • Your mental capacity checked
  • Properly insured
{{icon:lock}}

Integrity

  • Your information never shared without permission
  • No benefit from your Will, and no conflicts of interest
  • No refusal based on age, disability, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation or other protected characteristics
{{icon:award}}

Competency

  • Sound knowledge of the law on Wills
  • Advice that complies with law and tax rules
  • Your requests handled promptly

Concerns? Contact STEP at step.org/professional-standards or 020 7340 0500. Full Code: step.org/will-code.
