My philosophy

Why I do what I do

I'm passionate about helping people sort out their Wills and plan their estate, simply because I see the difference it makes to their peace of mind. It's quite common for people not to have a Will. It's one of those things they'll get around to “one of these days”, but often they're quietly worried about not having provided for their loved ones.

What makes me a little different is that I offer a friendly, no-obligation consultation in your own home, at work or online, in the evening or at weekends if that suits you better. I personally draft every document in house, which means a consistent level of service and nothing gets “lost in translation”.

Providing an affordable service that doesn't compromise on quality matters enormously to me. I'll take the time to go through every detail and answer every question, and no job is too big or too small. I've even been known to turn a Will around in a couple of hours in an emergency.

“What matters to me is that my clients are happy. And they are.”

Sevvy Hasan

My journey

  • 2012: Started by chance as a legal consultant, meeting clients on behalf of legal firms and solicitors.
  • Qualified: Studied for formal Will-writing qualifications, including STEP's Advanced Certificate in Will Preparation.
  • 2014: Founded King Will's Wills Ltd. The first office was on King William Street in Stourbridge, which is where the name comes from.
  • Today: Helping families across England and Wales, from Stourbridge and Worcester.
Qualified, regulated, insured

Peace of mind you can check

Will writing isn't regulated by law, so anyone can call themselves a Will writer. These are the standards I hold myself to.

{{icon:award}}

STEP Advanced Certificate in Will Preparation

Widely considered the highest qualification in Will writing. As a STEP Affiliate I'm bound by the STEP Code for Will Preparation, which sets out the transparency, integrity and competence you can expect.

{{icon:shieldcheck}}

Fellow of the Institute of Professional Willwriters

The IPW's strict code of practice is approved by the Chartered Trading Standards Institute. Entrance exams and continued study every year are mandatory.

{{icon:lock}}

Fully insured

£2 million professional indemnity insurance (upgradable to £5 million) and £5 million public liability insurance. All advice and documents are covered.

{{icon:scale}}

External complaints procedure

If you're ever unhappy, you have independent routes to complain through both the IPW and STEP. It's rare for DIY, online or bank Will services to offer this.
