Questions

Common questions

How much does a Will cost?

Every fee is fixed and quoted in full at your first appointment, before you decide anything. There are no hourly charges or hidden extras, and we'll match any like-for-like quote. Payment can be split, with a 50% deposit and the balance when your documents are returned.

Do you really come to my home?

Yes, at no extra cost, including evenings and weekends. If you'd prefer, we can meet over Zoom instead. We cover the whole of England and Wales.

Are you solicitors?

No. We're specialist, qualified Will writers. Sevvy holds STEP's Advanced Certificate in Will Preparation, widely regarded as the highest qualification in Will writing, and is a Fellow of the Institute of Professional Willwriters, whose code of practice is approved by the Chartered Trading Standards Institute. All advice and documents are fully insured.

How long does it take?

Usually two appointments: one to understand your wishes and one to sign. Because everything is drafted in house, turnaround is quick, and in an emergency it can be within two hours.

What if I already have a Will?

A Will should be reviewed every few years, and straight away after births, deaths, marriage, divorce, moving home or an inheritance. We're happy to review an existing Will and tell you honestly whether it still does the job.
