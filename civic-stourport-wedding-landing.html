Frequently asked

Honest answers, before you even ask.

Can we bring our own caterers?

Yes — you're free to use your own suppliers, or use our trusted local contacts.

Can we decorate the venue ourselves?

Absolutely. We offer flexible setup access so you can style the space your way.

Can children attend?

Yes — The Civic is set up for family-friendly receptions of all kinds.

Is there parking available?

Yes — there is convenient local parking available for guests.

Can we use our own DJ or entertainment?

Yes — external entertainment suppliers are welcome.

How many guests can you accommodate?

We can host wedding receptions for up to approximately 250 guests.

Can we access the venue early for setup?

Yes — day-before access is included where arranged in advance.

What's included in the venue hire?

Tables, chairs, bar staff, duty manager, security and venue access are all included.

Do you help with planning?

Yes — we're happy to guide couples through logistics, layouts and supplier coordination.
