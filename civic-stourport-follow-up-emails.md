# The Civic Stourport — follow-up email drafts

Drafted from the Caroline Caldwell call (12 Aug 2026). For use when building the
GHL automations/workflows for the wedding enquiry pipeline — not yet wired up
anywhere, just saved here so the copy doesn't get lost.

Pipeline these sit in, per the call: **enquiry form filled in → auto-reply
below → Caroline calls to arrange a visit → visit happens → they say yes →
confirmation email below sent, with booking form + T&Cs attached.**

---

## 1. Auto-reply — sent immediately when the enquiry form is submitted

Caroline was clear this should NOT read as "give me a call" — she can't
always get to the phone, so *she* calls *them*, and wants to avoid people
trying her three times in a day.

**Subject:** Thanks for your interest in The Civic

> Thank you for your interest in The Civic — we're so pleased you're
> thinking of us for your day.
>
> I'm sorry I can't get back to you the very second this comes through, but
> I will be in touch as soon as I can. I'll give you a call to arrange a
> suitable time for both of us — if there are times that don't work for
> you, just reply to this email and let me know, so I don't miss you.
>
> Looking forward to speaking soon.
>
> Caroline

---

## 2. Booking confirmation — sent once they've said yes on the phone/visit

Caroline wants this to be warm and simple: confirms the booking, asks only
for the 50% deposit against the **basic hire fee**, and says everything
else gets discussed at a later meeting. Booking form and T&Cs should be
attached to this email.

**Subject:** Your wedding reception at The Civic — next steps

> It was lovely meeting you the other day — we're really pleased you've
> chosen The Civic to host your wedding reception.
>
> All we need from you now is your confirmation booking form and your 50%
> deposit on the basic hire fee. Once that's in, we'll book in another
> meeting to sit down and go through everything else for your day.
>
> Your booking form and terms & conditions are attached.
>
> Looking forward to it.
>
> Caroline
