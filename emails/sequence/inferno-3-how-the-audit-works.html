A quick one on what an Inferno Audit actually involves.

A bigger business needs a bigger audit: more people, more time, more moving parts. So I come to you. I spend time with the people who run the business day to day and map how things really work:

→  Where your leads actually come from, and where the data trail breaks

→  The processes that live in someone's head or one spreadsheet

→  The tools you're paying for but not using to their fullest

→  The gaps nobody owns, and what they're costing you

You get a clear, honest picture of what's working, what's not, and what to do about it - in order.

There's no obligation to go further. The audit stands on its own. But if you want it, I stay as your embedded systems partner: building the fixes, training your team, and sticking around until the results land. I often work alongside existing consultants and agencies, too - I'm the implementation layer that turns recommendations into reality.

That comes from 25 years of doing exactly this inside large businesses and retail chains, long before automation was a buzzword.
