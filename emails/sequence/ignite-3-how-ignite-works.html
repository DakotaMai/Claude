A few people have asked what actually happens in Ignite, so here it is.

Ignite is your business audit. It's a 90-minute deep dive into how your business really runs, and you walk away with:

→  A complete map of your client journey, from first enquiry to signed client

→  Your pipeline and workflow design - how sales, onboarding and delivery should flow

→  A clear systems architecture - what to build, and how it all connects

→  A prioritised build plan, so everything happens in the right order

→  14 days of follow-up support while you review and refine
